# GreenCollar — Seven-Step Workflow

1. **Set up business and customers.** Business profile; names, addresses, contacts, photos, preferences, rates, services, schedules, vehicles, and equipment.
2. **Plan workday.** Operator sets start/end time or maximum jobs, constraints, fixed appointments, and flexible customers.
3. **Generate and approve route.** Select eligible visits, estimate durations, optimize real-road travel, estimate fuel and projected finish time; operator approves.
4. **Navigate and notify.** Show directions and customer details, remind operator of preferences; optionally send consent-based ETA SMS.
5. **Perform and track.** GPS-assisted arrival suggestion, manual timer overrides, notes, photos, on-site reminders and questions.
6. **Complete and bill.** Confirm completion, prepare approved text/photo update, create invoice and secure payment link, expose next-visit options.
7. **Learn and replan.** Compare planned and actual duration/travel/cost, improve predictions, adjust remaining schedule; ask approval before material changes.

Repeat steps 4–7 for each job.

## North star

Operator enters information once, approves the plan, and focuses on work while GreenCollar minimizes repetitive administration. Each visit improves future projections.

## Product audience

Any on-site service business: lawn care, pool cleaning, mobile detailing, cleaning, pest control, repair and more. Industry-specific service templates sit on a shared core.
